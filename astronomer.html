About

I'm a Master's student in Astronomy and Astrophysics at the University of Antananarivo, currently training in radio astronomy and VLBI data reduction through the DARA programme. My work sits between observational astronomy and scientific computing — I like turning messy datasets into something clear. Outside coursework, I build small Python tools and explore how machine learning could help with radio astronomy data, including MeerKAT.
